More options
Picture size
Higher is sharper but needs more from the phone and makes bigger files. 720p is a good start.
Orientation
Portrait is for vertical video (stories, phone screens).

Breathing zoom
Smarter matching (AI)
An AI model (CLIP) looks at what is in your loops and groups similar ones, which gives smoother sets. It downloads about 90 MB the first time (about 150 MB with a theme), so use Wi-Fi. It takes a little longer before the video starts. If the phone cannot run it, the app carries on without it.
Loops that match the theme are preferred. Needs smarter matching switched on.
Seed (optional)
The same seed and settings give the same mix. The seed of each export is shown on its card.
